# Expense Tracker — Privacy

Everything stays on your phone.

• The app reads bank and card SMS only after you allow it, and keeps the amount, date, bank, merchant and the last digits of the account or card. The SMS text itself is not kept.

• It never stores full card or account numbers, CVV, PIN, OTP or passwords.

• It has no account, no server, no ads and no tracking. It does not use the internet.

• Data leaves the phone only when you share a backup or export file yourself. Those files are not encrypted.

• Amounts on the home-screen widget are visible to anyone who sees your screen. Hide amounts or App lock hides them.

• Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored.
